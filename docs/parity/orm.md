## Known gaps and documented differences

- **ORM is SQLite-only**: `Session` binds a sync `Engine`/`Connection`; sync `create_engine` routes SQLite URLs only. No `AsyncSession` exists, so ORM behavior on PostgreSQL/MySQL is unverified.
- **Join inference is FK-metadata only**: non-secondary relationships always join on single-column `ForeignKey`s; `primaryjoin`/`secondaryjoin` passed there are silently ignored rather than rejected (deviation — SQLAlchemy honors them).
- **Unknown `lazy=` values degrade to `"select"`**: only `"select"` (proven) and `"raise"` (implemented, unproven) have distinct behavior; `"joined"`/`"selectin"`/`"subquery"`/`"immediate"`/`"noload"`/`"dynamic"`/`"write"` silently act as `"select"`.
- **`viewonly` is enforced for many-to-many only**; on one-to-many/many-to-one it does not prevent flush writes.
- **`refresh-expire` cascade token parses but never fires**: `Session.refresh` reloads column attributes and drops loaded relationship keys but does not expire/cascade to related objects.
- **Unsupported `relationship()` kwargs raise `ArgumentError`**: `order_by`, `collection_class`, `innerjoin`, `active_history`, `foreign_keys`, `remote_side`, `overlaps`, `foreign()`, `and_()` join clauses, `secondaryjoin` on non-secondary (accepted but ignored for `primaryjoin`/`secondaryjoin` only).
- **Post-update cycles** (dependency cycles requiring `post_update`) are unimplemented; flush orders by FK dependency and would fail or mis-order cyclic graphs.
- **Bulk ORM `delete()`/`update()` on JTI parents** emits single-table DML and orphans the other table's rows — verified SQLAlchemy surprise, reproduced intentionally (`tests/test_inheritance.py::test_jti_bulk_delete_parent_leaves_child_rows`).
- **`lazy="raise"` exists without a proving test** — implementation-only evidence; treated as unproven until a suite covers it.
- **`Mapped`/`mapped_column` differences**: `default=`/`default_factory=` also seed the generated `__init__` (plain `DeclarativeBase` leaves unset attributes alone in SQLAlchemy); non-`Mapped` annotations raise `ArgumentError`; bare `mapped_column()` maps with Column-default nullable.
